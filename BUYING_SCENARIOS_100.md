# 100 Real Enterprise Buying Scenarios (Role Psychology Lens)

**Purpose:** Understand how executives and operators think when operational economics break down.  
**Perspective included:** Manufacturing CEO, Retail CEO, CFO, COO, Procurement Director, HR Director, Plant Manager, Board Member.  
**Format per scenario:** What happened, first observer, blame pattern, current investigation method, why it fails, estimated loss, board narrative, trigger event for software approval.

## Scenarios 1–20: Profit, cash, and commercial contradictions

- **What happened?** Revenue rose 12%, EBIT fell 3 points due to discounts, expedite freight, and returns.

- **Money lost:** £8.5M/year.
- **Board discussion:** “Are we buying bad revenue?”
- **Approval trigger:** Second quarter repeat with guidance downgrade.

## Meta-patterns observed across 100 scenarios

1. **First signal usually appears in finance or operations, not in strategy decks.**
2. **Blame typically starts with one function, but root cause is cross-functional.**
3. **Current investigations are episodic and document-heavy, not continuous and causal.**
4. **Failure mode is consistent: local KPI success masks enterprise value destruction.**
5. **Board approval happens after repeat pattern + external pressure (covenant, customer, regulator, investor).**
