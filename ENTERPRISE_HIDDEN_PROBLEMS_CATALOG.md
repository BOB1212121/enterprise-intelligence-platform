# Enterprise Hidden Problems Catalogue (CTO/CEO Strategy Draft)

**Date:** 2026-07-27  
**Scope:** Medium/large ERP-using enterprises (initial focus: Gulf region)  
**Mode:** Problem analysis only — no solution proposals

---

## Strategic framing

We are not designing an ERP add-on. We are identifying expensive operational failures that organizations normalize as "business as usual," then selecting opportunities with **£1M+ annual impact per customer**.

## Strategic challenge note

To avoid becoming an ERP add-on, initial company wedge should target high-frequency, board-visible, economics-heavy pain where per-customer impact is routinely £1M+ and renewal value compounds via closed-loop learning.
